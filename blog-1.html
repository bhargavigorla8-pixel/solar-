What to Expect During Your Solar Installation

From the first site visit to switching on your solar system, here's a simple guide to what happens during a professional solar installation.

ST
SolarTech Team September 7, 2026 · Solar Energy Experts
Professional Solar Installation

Choosing solar is a major step toward cleaner, more reliable energy. But what actually happens after you decide to install a solar system? Understanding the process can make the experience easier and help you know what to expect at every stage.

01

It starts with a site assessment

Before any panels are installed, our team evaluates your property and energy requirements. This includes reviewing your roof, available installation space, electrical setup, shading, and expected energy usage.

This assessment helps determine the right system size, panel placement, inverter configuration, and overall installation approach for your property.

What our team checks

Roof condition, orientation, shading, electrical infrastructure, energy usage, and available installation space.

02

Your system is designed around your needs

Once the assessment is complete, your solar system is designed around your property's energy profile. The goal is not simply to add panels, but to create a system that works efficiently for your daily electricity requirements.

Your proposal may include solar panels, an inverter, mounting equipment, monitoring technology, and optional battery storage depending on your goals.

A system designed for your property
03

Installation day

On installation day, the SolarTech team arrives prepared with the equipment and materials required for the project. Our installers begin by preparing the work area and confirming the approved layout.

Mounting hardware is secured first. Solar panels are then positioned and connected, followed by the inverter and electrical connections. Every component is installed according to the planned system design.

01

Site preparation

The installation area is prepared and safety checks are completed.

02

Panel installation

Panels and mounting systems are securely installed.

03

Electrical setup

The inverter and electrical system are connected and tested.

04

Final inspection

The completed system is checked before activation.

04

Testing and system commissioning

After installation, your system goes through a series of checks. The team verifies electrical connections, inverter operation, panel performance, monitoring communication, and system safety.

Once everything has been confirmed, the solar system is ready for the next stage of activation and monitoring.

A good solar installation is not just about putting panels on a roof. It is about creating an energy system that performs reliably for years to come.
— SolarTech Installation Team
05

Monitoring your solar system

Your installation does not end when the equipment is switched on. Monitoring allows you to understand how much energy your system is producing and how effectively it is performing.

Depending on your selected system, monitoring tools can help you review energy generation, system status, historical performance, and potential issues.

  • Track solar energy generation
  • Monitor system performance
  • Identify unusual system behavior
  • Understand your energy savings
06

What happens after installation?

Once your solar system is operational, you can begin generating clean electricity and tracking its performance. SolarTech also provides ongoing support to help you keep your system operating efficiently.

Regular monitoring and appropriate maintenance can help protect your investment and ensure your solar system continues performing as expected.

Our solar specialists can help you understand your energy needs, system options, installation timeline, and next steps.
