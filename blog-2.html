What to Expect During Your Solar Installation

Thinking about going solar? From the initial site assessment to switching on your new system, here's exactly what you can expect during your solar installation journey.

ST
SolarTech Team Solar Energy Specialists
Published September 08, 2026
Reading time 7 min read
CLEAN ENERGY Powering a brighter tomorrow.

Installing solar panels is one of the most valuable upgrades you can make to your property. But if you've never gone through the process before, you may be wondering what actually happens once you've decided to make the switch.

A professional solar installation is a carefully planned process involving site assessment, system design, permits, equipment installation, electrical connections and final inspections. The good news is that your solar provider handles most of the technical work for you.

01. Initial Site Assessment

The first step is understanding your property and energy requirements. Our solar specialists evaluate your roof, available sunlight, electrical infrastructure and current electricity usage.

What we check
  • Roof condition and available space
  • Sunlight exposure and shading
  • Current electricity consumption
  • Electrical panel capacity

02. Solar System Design

Once the site assessment is complete, your installer creates a customized solar system designed around your energy needs. Panel placement, inverter selection, system capacity and future energy requirements are all considered.

The goal isn't simply to install as many panels as possible. A good solar design maximizes energy production while making efficient use of your available space.

A well-designed solar system is an investment in your property's future — not just a way to reduce today's electricity bill.

03. Permits & Approvals

Depending on your location and utility provider, certain permits and approvals may be required before installation can begin. Your solar installation company typically coordinates this process and makes sure the system meets the required safety and electrical standards.

01
Documentation

System plans and technical documentation are prepared.

02
Permit Submission

Required applications are submitted to the relevant authorities.

03
Approval

Installation proceeds once the necessary approvals are received.

04. Installation Day

Installation day is when your solar project starts becoming a reality. A professional installation team arrives with the required equipment and begins preparing your property.

Depending on the size and complexity of your system, installation may take anywhere from several hours to a few days.

Mounting Solar mounting structures are securely installed.
Panels Solar panels are positioned for optimal sunlight.
Wiring Electrical connections are carefully completed.
Inverter The inverter connects solar generation to your electrical system.

05. Inspection & System Activation

After installation, the system goes through final checks to ensure everything is connected correctly and operating safely.

Once the required inspection and utility approvals are complete, your solar system can officially begin generating clean energy.

You're officially generating solar energy.

Your monitoring system will allow you to track production and understand how your solar investment is performing.

What Happens After Installation?

Your relationship with your solar provider doesn't necessarily end when the panels are switched on. Depending on your service agreement, you may have access to system monitoring, maintenance support and performance assistance.

Ready to Make the Switch?

Solar installation doesn't have to be complicated. With the right installation partner, every stage can be carefully planned and professionally managed. From the first assessment to the moment your system starts producing clean energy, the goal is simple: make your transition to solar as smooth as possible.
